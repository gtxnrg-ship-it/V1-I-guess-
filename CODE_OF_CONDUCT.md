# Code of Conduct

Kuro-chan is a community project for people with different backgrounds, skill levels, and resources. We want participation to be welcoming, constructive, and safe.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being kind, patient, and respectful.
- Giving constructive feedback and accepting it gracefully.
- Explaining disagreements without personal attacks.
- Helping newcomers learn and participate.
- Respecting privacy and not sharing another person's secrets, credentials, or private work.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention without consent, deliberate intimidation, and publishing private information without permission.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or decline contributions that violate this code and may block participants who repeatedly or seriously violate it. Decisions should be proportionate to the behavior and focused on protecting the community.

## Reporting a concern

Please contact the project maintainer through the repository's GitHub profile or use GitHub's private reporting options where available. For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of opening a public issue.

This policy applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and project-related communication.
